Quantitative Finance / Trading

Prop Firm Statistical Risk Dashboard

A statistical risk-management framework designed to evaluate the sustainability of futures trading strategies within proprietary trading firm account constraints. The project focuses on how trade-level performance, payout structures, and account drawdown limits interact to influence long-term trading outcomes.

Analytical Approach

  • Evaluate trade-level win rates, average winning and losing trades, and risk-reward relationships.
  • Calculate expected value per trade using probability-weighted outcomes.
  • Analyze the effect of position sizing on account drawdowns and risk exposure.
  • Explore Monte Carlo simulation as a method for estimating possible equity paths and losing streaks.

Key Financial Concepts

  • Expected value: EV = (Win Rate × Avg. Win) − (Loss Rate × Avg. Loss).
  • Risk of ruin and account survival under different trading assumptions.
  • Drawdown sensitivity and capital allocation across trading accounts.
  • Performance evaluation beyond gross profits, including consistency and downside exposure.

Corporate Finance / Internship

Chobani Price-Volume-Mix (PVM) Analysis

During my corporate finance internship at Chobani, I worked on price-volume-mix analysis to better understand the underlying drivers of revenue performance. PVM analysis separates revenue movements into changes attributable to product pricing, sales volume, and shifts in the mix of products sold.

This approach provides a more meaningful explanation of revenue performance than looking at total sales growth alone, helping distinguish pricing improvements from changes in consumer demand and product composition.

Analytical Approach

  • Organize revenue and unit-sales information at the product or SKU level.
  • Compare financial performance across reporting periods.
  • Separate price, volume, and mix effects to explain revenue movements.
  • Identify product-level contributions to changes in overall revenue.

Business Applications

  • Distinguish revenue growth driven by higher prices from growth driven by additional unit sales.
  • Understand how product assortment affects consolidated performance.
  • Support budgeting, forecasting, and commercial performance reviews.
  • Communicate financial results through structured variance analysis.
